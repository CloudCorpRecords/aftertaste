Aftertaste

A date-night agent with taste. Tell it what they love — an artist, a film — and it plans the whole night in their taste language: dinner, a film, the walk-home soundtrack, a nightcap. Every pick shows the taste thread that connects it to them, measured in Qloo's taste graph, not guessed by a language model.

Powered by the Qloo Taste AI API (250M+ cultural entities). “Without Qloo” ranks by raw popularity alone — the generic-agent baseline. Build Disclosure: AI-built by the Tree / Muse agent fleet, directed by Rene Turcios.
